    About Jatri Seba

    What this platform does

    Jatri Seba helps travellers in Bangladesh discover bus, train, launch, bike and private-car services, compare routes, sample fares, journey times and ratings, plan multi-transport journeys on a map, and message other travellers. This is a Stage 1 interactive prototype: all schedules, fares and ratings are sample data, clearly labelled, and no real tickets, accounts or payments are processed.

    How route information and ratings work

    Routes come from the sample dataset shown on this page. “Best Suggestion” scores each option by normalising fare, journey time and rating to a 0–100 scale and shows the breakdown, so you can see why an option was recommended. Ratings are a 1–5 star average with a review count; in this prototype a rating is stored only in your browser session and each service can be rated once per session.
